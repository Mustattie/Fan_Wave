## Review-derived focus items (from the P3.6 code review of ae9cded..HEAD)

Headline of the review: no Phase 2/3 change touched the two mechanisms behind
the S10+ OOM (buffer option values are byte-identical; one player, one
VideoView). It found one new unbounded resource and two pre-existing players
without buffer caps; all three are fixed in v9.5.35 and must be verified on
the device below.

| # | Item (fixed in v9.5.35) | Reproduce | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
